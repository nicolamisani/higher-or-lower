# Credits

## Photographs

All five are **CC0 1.0 Universal** — the creators waived their rights
worldwide, so no attribution is legally required and the images can be used
commercially, modified and redistributed. They are credited here anyway,
because it costs nothing and because the next person to touch this app should
be able to check the licence without re-deriving it.

The licence of each was read from the hosting API's own metadata, not inferred
from a search result.

Found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org/) and Wikimedia Commons,
filtering to CC0 and public domain only. Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay were not
used: their images are free to use but are **not** public domain, and each
carries its own licence with its own conditions. Staying on CC0 means the app
can be published, copied, handed to a colleague or reused next year with
nothing to check.

Originals are kept in `photos/`. They are centre-cropped, resized and embedded
as data URIs by `embed_photos.py` — the published page makes no external image
request.

## Data

Climate-change figures are **AGRIBALYSE® 3.2** (ADEME), indicator
*Changement climatique*, kg CO₂e per kg of product, scaled to an 80 g portion.
Published under the **Licence Ouverte / Open Licence** (Etalab), which permits
reuse including commercially, with attribution to ADEME.

Source: <https://data.ademe.fr/datasets/agribalyse-31-synthese>

The 80 g portion is the standard vegetable serving used by the Association of
UK Dietitians, which is also the portion basis the Financial Times used for its
carbon-labelling interactive — so the two are directly comparable.
