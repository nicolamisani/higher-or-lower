# Credits

## Photographs

**48 of the 51 panels carry a photograph.** Every one is **CC0 1.0** or
**public domain**: the licence filter was applied at the API, and each record's
licence field is reproduced below rather than inferred from a search result.
Attribution is not legally required for any of them; it is given anyway.

Sourced through [Openverse](https://openverse.org/), which aggregates StockSnap,
Rawpixel, Wikimedia Commons and others. Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay were not
used: their images are free to use but are **not** public domain, and each
carries its own conditions that would have to be re-checked every time this app
is copied or reused.

**Butter, Cheddar, Lentils have no photograph** and fall back to the flat colour of their food group.
That is a supported state, not a missing asset — but they are the obvious place
to improve the set, and dropping a file named after the food into `photos/` and
re-running `embed_photos.py` is all it takes.

## Data

Climate-change figures are **AGRIBALYSE® 3.2** (ADEME), indicator
*Changement climatique*, kg CO₂e per kg of product, scaled to an 80 g portion.
Published under the **Licence Ouverte / Open Licence** (Etalab), which permits
reuse including commercially, with attribution to ADEME.

Source: <https://data.ademe.fr/datasets/agribalyse-31-synthese>

The 80 g portion is the standard vegetable serving used by the Association of
UK Dietitians, which is also the portion basis the Financial Times used for its
carbon-labelling interactive — so the two are directly comparable.
