because every photograph is cropped and brightened at build time, which makes
the version in the app an adaptation, so that adapted image stays under
CC BY-NC-SA 2.0. Replacing it with a CC0 or CC BY photograph would remove the
only obligation attached to this app; nothing else here constrains reuse.

## Data

Climate-change figures are **AGRIBALYSE® 3.2** (ADEME), indicator
*Changement climatique*, kg CO₂e per kg of product, scaled to an 80 g portion.
Published under the **Licence Ouverte / Open Licence** (Etalab), which permits
reuse including commercially, with attribution to ADEME.

Source: <https://data.ademe.fr/datasets/agribalyse-31-synthese>

The 80 g portion is the standard vegetable serving used by the Association of
UK Dietitians, which is also the portion basis the Financial Times used for its
carbon-labelling interactive — so the two are directly comparable.
